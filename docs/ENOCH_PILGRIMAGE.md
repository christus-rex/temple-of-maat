# Enoch — The Angelic Mirror Pilgrimage

This route is the first named Temple pilgrimage layered alongside the existing 72-chamber Pilgrim Journey. It does **not** replace the chamber journey and does not write personal material into the public Relationship Graph or Knowledge Kernel.

## Purpose

The route gives the visitor a private place to explore personal Enochian experiences while preserving the Temple's source and discernment boundaries.

Its visible progression is:

`SOURCE → EXPERIENCE → INTERPRETATION → VERIFICATION → CONDUCT`

Each gate uses the private Reality Record:

`Observation → Interpretation → Verification → Conduct`

The first field records what happened before meaning is assigned. The second records current meaning without requiring exclusivity. The third asks what can support, challenge, or qualify the interpretation. The fourth returns the experience to proportionate action and ordinary responsibility.

## Source basis

The route is grounded in the project volume *The Angelic Mirror — A Personal Enochian Sourcebook, Ritual Compendium, Gematria and Divinatory Reading*.

That volume explicitly separates four authority levels:

- **HISTORICAL** — material anchored in Dee–Kelley papers or close early witnesses;
- **RECONSTRUCTED** — later systems built from the papers, especially major occult reorganizations;
- **MODERN** — contemporary techniques inspired by the historical material;
- **PERSONAL** — transparent symbolic applications created for the reader.

The route preserves those labels mechanically in its data and visibly in the interface.

The route also retains the sourcebook's distinction between the ancient/apocalyptic figure Enoch and the later body commonly called Enochian. It never treats the Book of Enoch and the Dee–Kelley archive as one historical corpus.

## Eight-gate form

The eight-gate structure is **PERSONAL / MODERN**, not historical Enochian architecture. It follows the sourcebook's disclosed use of 8 as a personal symbolic index.

1. **The Covenant** — authority levels and preserved uncertainty.
2. **The Archive** — Enoch, Dee, Kelley, and source boundaries.
3. **The Angelical Word** — language, transliteration, pronunciation, accountable speech.
4. **Sevenfold Governance** — Heptarchia, virtue, shadow, and lawful power.
5. **The Four Watchtowers** — Air / Water / Earth / Fire as a modern ethical examination.
6. **The Aethyric Threshold** — thirty Aethyrs and ninety-one governors; source literacy before ascent.
7. **ZID — The Balanced Voice** — the disclosed personal indexing of ZID, TODNAON, PRISTAC, and ZAMFRES.
8. **Embodied Verification** — audit the record and choose PROCEED, PAUSE, or STUDY FURTHER.

## Personal-state boundary

State is stored under:

`temple_pilgrimage_enoch_v1`

The state contract is `temple-of-maat/pilgrimage-state-v1` and contains only route progress plus the four private Reality Record fields for each gate. It remains device-local unless the visitor explicitly chooses **Download Enoch Route JSON**.

The route never serializes personal observations, interpretations, or reflections into:

- `research/knowledge-kernel/*`;
- `research/relationship-graph.json`;
- `library/catalog.json`;
- any public chamber record.

## Safety and grounding covenant

The route is contemplative and non-coercive. It excludes instructions that encourage harm, threats, isolation, sleep deprivation, intoxicant-driven practice, compulsive repetition, reckless spending, abandonment of care, or claims that personal experience grants exceptional rank.

The first-month gate explicitly does not instruct a full Aethyr ascent. Visionary material is recorded rather than forced. If engagement worsens sleep, work, relationships, fear, compulsion, grandiosity, or safety, the route instructs the visitor to pause and return to ordinary grounding and trusted support.

## Release invariants

The route must preserve:

- the manual Temple threshold;
- the existing 72-chamber Journey and its local state;
- no ritual-media autoplay;
- the seven-control mobile dock geometry;
- mobile usability at 360 px and 412 px widths;
- source / reconstruction / modern / personal distinctions;
- device-local personal state;
- no public graph or Knowledge Kernel leakage.

The Enoch route is a progressive enhancement. If its data cannot load, the core Temple and existing Journey remain usable.
