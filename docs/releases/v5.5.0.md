# Temple v5.5.0 — The Living Archive

Build: `2026-08-18-v5.5-living-archive`

## Purpose

v5.5 turns the Temple's growing body of chambers, source studies, poems, artwork collections, ritual audio, gematria archives, and preservation records into a unified discoverable archive without replacing the existing 72-chamber experience or the dedicated Poems Chamber and Temple Library.

## Additions

- Living Archive unified search across `chambers.json`, the public Temple Library catalog, and the v5.5 preservation manifest.
- Search filters for chambers, poems, text, artwork, audio, gematria, sources, studies, discernment, correspondences, and traditions.
- Local-only favorites and recently viewed records.
- `Ctrl/Cmd + K` archive shortcut after entering the Temple.
- Direct routing into chambers, the Poems Chamber, Temple Library records, high-fidelity Drive sources, ritual audio, and the Shem 72 archive.
- Google Drive preservation root with dedicated Artwork, Audio, Text/PDF, Release Backup, and Manifest/Provenance folders.
- Machine-readable preservation metadata in `data/living-archive-v5.5.json`.
- Automated v5.5 archive contract validation.
- Preserved v5.4.0 rollback branch and release documentation.

## Preservation boundary

Large masters remain in Drive. The website carries lightweight public renditions, structured metadata, searchable records, and links to the preserved originals. Provenance layers remain explicit: L1 source, L2 documented scholarship/editorial context, L3 comparison, L4 symbolic/original Temple synthesis.
