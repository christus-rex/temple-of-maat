ABOUT • METHODOLOGY • PROVENANCE

Discern the source layer.

The Temple deliberately distinguishes documented historical reference from later occult manuscript tradition and from its own modern symbolic synthesis.

HISTORICAL EGYPTIAN REFERENCE

Ma’at

Ancient Egyptian material provides the ethical and visual reference for Ma’at: right order, truth, justice, balance, the feather, and the weighing of the heart. The Temple uses these as historical reference while its chamber architecture remains modern.

MANUSCRIPT / OCCULT-HISTORY REFERENCE

Solomonic traditions

Texts attributed to Solomon survive in medieval and early-modern manuscript traditions. The Temple draws names and visual ideas from that broad stream without presenting its own 72-chamber synthesis as an ancient Egyptian or historically Solomonic system.

ORIGINAL TEMPLE SYNTHESIS

The 72 chambers

The angel ↔ daemon pairings, reconciled third names, seven Fires, Offices, Laws, threshold architecture, and human-origin seals are original creative constructions of this project. They are symbolic correspondences, not archaeological claims.

SEAL GRAMMAR

Human origin at the center

Every v6.2 seal keeps a human figure central. Seven Fire motifs encode the Fire, three structural arrangements encode Jachin, Boaz, or Middle, and a deterministic chamber mark differentiates each of the 72 seals.
