# Source Notes — Temple of Ma’at v6.2.1

## Source classes

1. **Historical Egyptian reference** — Ma’at, feather, right order/truth/justice, weighing imagery.
2. **Manuscript / occult-history reference** — later Solomonic/Clavicula/Lemegeton traditions.
3. **Temple synthesis** — the 72 pairings, third names, seven Fires, Offices, Laws, threshold system, and human-origin seals.

## Selected reference records

- The Metropolitan Museum of Art, *Figure of Maat on a pedestal*, object 07.228.30.
- British Museum, figure of Ma’at, registration 1946,1204.22.a.
- British Library, Add MS 36674, Key of Knowledge / Clavicula tradition.
- British Library, Sloane MS 3648, Lesser Key / Lemegeton tradition.

## Editorial rule

No Temple-original pairing, chamber Law, third name, Fire architecture, or generated seal is described as an ancient Egyptian artifact or a historically transmitted Solomonic seal unless independent evidence is added.
